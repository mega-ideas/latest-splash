HOW IT WORKS

One payment, start to finish

1

You choose who you're paying

A business bank account, a business on Splash, or a Sui wallet address.

2

Splash checks the recipient

Bank details against what PH or ID banks require. Wallet addresses against sanctions lists.

3

The money comes from your Splash Wallet

Your business holds the keys. Splash never holds your funds.

4

A second person approves

The rate locks for 5 minutes and your approver signs on their own device.

5

One Sui transaction pays and records it

The payment, its record and its receipt happen together, or not at all. For a bank payout, our licensed partner then credits the account by InstaPay, PESONet or BI-FAST.
